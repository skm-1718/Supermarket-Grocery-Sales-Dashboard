## 🏢 Project Overview

The **Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard** is an end-to-end retail analytics project developed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product contribution, discount behavior, and geographic trends.

The project transforms raw grocery sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and an interactive Excel dashboard.

The dashboard enables management to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify high-performing product categories, evaluate profitability, and understand regional sales distribution for better business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

### 📌 Objective 1: Overall Sales Performance
- Calculate Total Sales and Total Profit.
- Identify categories contributing the largest share of sales.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Determine the highest-performing sales regions.

### 🛍️ Objective 2: Product Performance
- Identify top-performing categories and sub-categories.
- Compare sales and profit contribution.
- Detect products with high sales but comparatively low profit.

### 💰 Objective 3: Profit & Discount Analysis
- Evaluate profit contribution across categories and regions.
- Analyze the relationship between discount and profit.
- Identify opportunities for improving profitability.

### 🌍 Objective 4: Geographic Performance
- Identify states and cities generating the highest sales.
- Compare sales and profit across locations.
- Identify high-sales locations with comparatively low profitability.

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The following data preparation activities were performed:

- Standardized inconsistent date formats into DD-MM-YYYY.
- Converted slash-separated dates into the correct day-month-year order.
- Verified numerical fields for sales, discount, and profit.
- Organized the dataset for Pivot Table analysis.
- Prepared category, regional, monthly, and geographic summaries.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 🥇 Category Performance
- Eggs, Meat & Fish generated the highest sales of ₹22,67,401.
- Snacks recorded the highest total profit of ₹5,68,178.85.
- Oil & Masala recorded the lowest category sales of ₹20,38,442.

### 🥤 Product Performance
- Health Drinks emerged as the highest-selling sub-category, generating ₹10,51,439 in sales.
- Spices recorded a comparatively low profit margin of approximately 23.82%.

### 🌍 Regional Performance
- West was the highest-performing region, generating ₹47,98,743 in sales.
- East followed with ₹42,48,368 in sales.
- The dataset contains only Tamil Nadu as a state, so a multi-state comparison is not possible.

### 💹 Discount & Profit Relationship
Pearson correlation analysis produced a value approximately equal to zero, indicating a negligible linear relationship between discount and profit in the observed records.

This does not establish that discounts have no causal impact on profitability.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- **Product Strategy:** Prioritize high-performing categories while evaluating profit margins.
- **Pricing Strategy:** Review pricing and procurement costs for comparatively low-margin sub-categories.
- **Regional Strategy:** Focus on West and East for regional performance analysis.
- **Discount Optimization:** Evaluate discount effectiveness using profitability metrics rather than sales volume alone.
- **Inventory Planning:** Use monthly sales trends to improve demand forecasting and stock allocation.
- **Data-Driven Decisions:** Continuously monitor KPIs through interactive dashboards.
